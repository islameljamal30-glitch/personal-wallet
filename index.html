Your bank balance shows what’s in your account. It doesn’t know your rent is due next week. Personal Wallet sets aside your bills and savings first, so the number you see is the number you can use.

Personal Wallet splits your money into what’s safe to spend, what you’ve set aside, and what’s reserved for bills. Only the first part is yours to spend.

Money you can’t touch yet

A blocked account, handled.

Add a blocked account, like a Sperrkonto, once: what’s in it and what it pays out each month. Personal Wallet keeps that money apart and releases each payout on schedule, so Safe to spend only counts money you can use.
