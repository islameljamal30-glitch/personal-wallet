N26: download your transactions as a CSV file

Checked on 19 November 2026 against Personal Wallet’s file reader.

Steps

N26 offers the CSV download on its web version. The statements in the N26 app are PDFs, which Personal Wallet can’t read.

  1. Log in to N26 in a web browser.
  2. On the Home tab, select your account (or a Space), then Downloads.
  3. Under Account Activity, enter a start and an end date.
  4. Choose CSV and select Download.
  5. Save the file. On a computer, you can send it to your iPhone with AirDrop or save it to iCloud Drive.

What Personal Wallet reads from it

Personal Wallet recognises N26’s CSV files on its own, in English and in German, and reads the date and the amount in euros. In our test, a payment made in another currency came in with its euro amount from the file.

One thing to know: in some N26 files, including the German ones, card payments arrive without the shop’s name and show as “Bank transaction”. You can still choose a category for each one before you add it.

Bringing it in

In Personal Wallet, choose Bring in your bank’s transactions and pick the file. It’s read on your iPhone only, with no bank login. You check every line, choose categories, and nothing is added until you confirm. Your first import is free. More imports come with Personal Wallet Pro.

Personal Wallet is coming soon to iPhone.

Good to know

  • The file contains account numbers and payment details. Delete it from your downloads when you’re done.
  • Never share your N26 login or confirmation codes with any app. Personal Wallet never asks for them.
