ING: download your transactions as a CSV file

For ING in Germany. Checked on 19 November 2026 against Personal Wallet’s file reader.

Steps

  1. Log in to ING online banking in a web browser.
  2. Open the account and its transactions (Umsätze).
  3. Choose the dates you want, if you don’t want everything shown.
  4. At the bottom of the list, choose Exportieren. If you’re asked for a format, choose CSV.
  5. Save the file. On a computer, you can send it to your iPhone with AirDrop or save it to iCloud Drive.

ING’s file says it contains only booked transactions, so pending ones (vorgemerkt) may be missing until they’re booked.

What Personal Wallet reads from it

ING’s file starts with a few lines about the account (IBAN, period, balance). Personal Wallet skips those and finds the transaction list on its own. It reads the date (Valuta), the amount (Betrag) and the payment text (Verwendungszweck).

Bringing it in

In Personal Wallet, choose Bring in your bank’s transactions and pick the file. It’s read on your iPhone only, with no bank login. You check every line, choose categories, and nothing is added until you confirm. Your first import is free. More imports come with Personal Wallet Pro.

Personal Wallet is coming soon to iPhone.

Good to know

  • The file contains your IBAN and payment details. Delete it from your downloads when you’re done.
  • Never share your online banking login or TANs with any app. Personal Wallet never asks for them.
