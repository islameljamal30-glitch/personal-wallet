Revolut: download your transactions as a CSV file

For personal Revolut accounts. Checked on 19 November 2026 against Personal Wallet’s file reader.

Steps

  1. Open the Revolut app and go to Home.
  2. Tap Accounts under your balance and choose the currency account you want, for example EUR.
  3. Tap More (…), then Statement.
  4. Choose the period, pick Excel as the format, and tap Generate.
  5. Save the file to Files on your iPhone.

Revolut’s “Excel” statement is usually a CSV file, which is what Personal Wallet reads. If the file you get ends in .csv, you’re set. A PDF statement won’t work. Menus can change, so the names may differ a little.

What Personal Wallet reads from it

Personal Wallet recognises Revolut’s file on its own. It reads the day each payment completed, the amount and the description, such as the shop’s name.

  • Payments Revolut marks as declined, reverted or still pending are left out, and Personal Wallet tells you how many. Pending ones come in with a later file once they’re completed.
  • Revolut makes one statement per currency. Choose the one in your account’s currency. Lines in another currency are shown, but not added.
  • Fees that Revolut lists in their own column aren’t added to the payment. If you paid a fee, add it by hand.

Bringing it in

In Personal Wallet, choose Bring in your bank’s transactions and pick the file. It’s read on your iPhone only, with no bank login. You check every line, choose categories, and nothing is added until you confirm. Your first import is free. More imports come with Personal Wallet Pro.

Personal Wallet is coming soon to iPhone.

Good to know

  • The file contains your payment details. Delete it from Files when you’re done.
  • Never share your Revolut passcode or confirmation codes with any app. Personal Wallet never asks for them.
