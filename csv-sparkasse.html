Sparkasse: download your transactions as a CSV file

Checked on 19 November 2026 against Personal Wallet’s file reader.

Steps

  1. Log in to your Sparkasse’s online banking in a web browser.
  2. Open your current account and its transactions (Umsätze).
  3. Choose the dates you want.
  4. Choose Export and pick the format CSV-CAMT V2. It may be listed as “Excel (CSV-CAMT V2)”.
  5. Save the file. On a computer, you can send it to your iPhone with AirDrop or save it to iCloud Drive.

Each Sparkasse runs its own online banking, so buttons can look a little different. Look for the export or download option next to your transaction list.

What Personal Wallet reads from it

Personal Wallet recognises the Sparkasse CSV-CAMT V2 file on its own: the date (Buchungstag), the amount (Betrag) and who you paid or who paid you (Begünstigter/Zahlungspflichtiger). German amounts like 1.234,56 and dates like 03.11.26 are read correctly.

Bringing it in

In Personal Wallet, choose Bring in your bank’s transactions and pick the file. It’s read on your iPhone only, with no bank login. You check every line, choose categories, and nothing is added until you confirm. Your first import is free. More imports come with Personal Wallet Pro.

Personal Wallet is coming soon to iPhone.

Good to know

  • The file contains your IBAN and payment details. Delete it from your downloads when you’re done.
  • Never share your online banking PIN or TANs with any app. Personal Wallet never asks for them.
