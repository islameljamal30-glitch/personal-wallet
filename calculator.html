How it’s worked out

Safe to spend = the money in your everyday account − the bills still to pay this month − the savings you still want to put aside − your buffer.

If that comes out below zero, you’ll see how much more you need to cover it, not a negative number to spend.

Why money you expect isn’t counted

A salary or a transfer that hasn’t arrived yet can be late or smaller than you think. Counting it early is an easy way for a budget to go wrong at the end of the month. So the number only uses money you already have.

Keep the number up to date
