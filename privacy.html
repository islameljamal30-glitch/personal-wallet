Privacy Policy

Personal Wallet for iPhone · Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version

Your wallet stays on your iPhone. Personal Wallet has no accounts, no servers of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. I, the developer, cannot see your entries, balances or plans.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you enter: accounts, entries, categories, budgets, bills, savings goals, plans, notes and settings. It is stored in the app's private storage on your device. The Personal Wallet widgets read a copy of the figures they show from storage that only this app and its widgets can open, on the same device.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. If you delete the app, iOS deletes this data with it.

Your iPhone may include the app's data in device backups you make with iCloud or a computer. Those backups are handled by Apple and by your own settings, not by Personal Wallet.

Backups and exports you make

You can export a full backup or a CSV list of transactions. The app only creates these files when you ask, and you choose where they go, for example Files, AirDrop or email. You can protect a backup with a password. It is then encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the key is derived from your password. Without the password nobody can read it, and that includes me, so I can't recover a lost password. A CSV file or an unprotected backup can be read by anyone who has the file.

Purchases (Personal Wallet Pro)

Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, billing, refunds and your Apple ID. I never receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app receives a record signed by Apple that says whether Pro is active, and checks it on your device. Apple sends me sales reports that don't identify you. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Features that use iOS

  • Face ID or passcode lock (optional): iOS checks your face or passcode. The app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives biometric data.
  • Reminders and notifications: these are scheduled on your iPhone. There is no notification server.
  • Siri, Shortcuts and widgets: "Log expense" and the widgets run on your device. If you use Siri, Apple processes your voice request under Apple's own terms and your Siri settings.
  • Crash reports: if you chose "Share with App Developers" in iOS settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash and usage reports. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

If you contact me

If you email me, I use your email address and message only to answer you. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR (answering a request about the app) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering questions). I delete the conversation once your request is settled, unless the law requires me to keep it. Please don't send bank passwords, PINs, card numbers or your wallet data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Because your wallet data never leaves your device, you hold it yourself: you can see, change, export or delete it in the app at any time. For anything I hold, such as an email you sent me, write to the address above.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. Mine is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin (datenschutz-berlin.de).

Changes

If the app changes how it handles data, for example if a later version offers optional sign-in and sync, I will update this policy before that version is released. The date at the top shows the latest version.
