Download your bank’s transactions as a CSV file

A CSV file is a simple list of your transactions that your online banking can give you. You can bring it into Personal Wallet instead of typing everything in. It’s read on your iPhone, and nothing is added until you check it.

  • SparkasseExport your Umsätze as CSV-CAMT V2.
  • INGExport from the transaction list in online banking.
  • N26Download Account Activity on N26 in a web browser.
  • DKBExport your Umsätze from DKB Banking.
  • RevolutDownload a statement in the Revolut app.
  • Other banksWhat your bank’s file needs, and how to find it.
