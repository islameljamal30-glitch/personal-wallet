DKB: download your transactions as a CSV file

For the DKB Banking introduced in 2023. Checked on 19 November 2026 against Personal Wallet’s file reader.

Steps

  1. Log in to DKB Banking in a web browser.
  2. Open your current account (Girokonto) and its transactions (Umsätze).
  3. If you can, filter the list to the dates you want.
  4. Choose Exportieren. If you’re offered a format, choose CSV. A PDF won’t work.
  5. Save the file. On a computer, you can send it to your iPhone with AirDrop or save it to iCloud Drive.

Menus can change. If you only see a PDF option, DKB may not offer CSV there; try DKB Banking in a web browser.

What Personal Wallet reads from it

DKB’s file starts with a few lines about the account. Personal Wallet skips those and finds the transactions on its own. It reads the booking date, the amount in euros and the other party: who paid you for money in, who you paid for money out.

Payments DKB marks as pending (vorgemerkt) are left out, so nothing is counted twice. They come in with a later file once they’re booked.

Bringing it in

In Personal Wallet, choose Bring in your bank’s transactions and pick the file. It’s read on your iPhone only, with no bank login. You check every line, choose categories, and nothing is added until you confirm. Your first import is free. More imports come with Personal Wallet Pro.

Personal Wallet is coming soon to iPhone.

Good to know

  • The file contains your IBAN and payment details. Delete it from your downloads when you’re done.
  • Never share your banking login or confirmation codes with any app. Personal Wallet never asks for them.
